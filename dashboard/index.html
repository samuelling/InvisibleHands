How the count works

  • A room reports to the meter at most once a minute, and only after something happened: a move, a join, a leave. A room where nothing moves costs nothing.
  • The libraries send a value only when it changes and pack moves made together into one frame.
  • Rooms sleep between messages, so time costs next to nothing; the bill is in requests.

The guard rails

  • Past the monthly ceiling, the relay closes until the 1st (editor keys excepted). Raise MONTHLY_FRAMES in wrangler.toml and redeploy to open it.
  • 30 new connections a minute per address; floods are cut off.
  • The request estimate is frames ÷ 20, plus a request per new connection, plus two per room report. Cloudflare’s own usage page has the exact figure.
